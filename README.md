# Data-Dye-Pack
Trapping AI hackers by deploying an enticing dummy file system to bait autonomous agents to look through your fake files. Upon scanning the file, it instantly triggers an encryption on your actual sensitive data and feeds the AI encrypted data it can’t digest thereby stopping company secrets from being compromised.

Let them dig. Everything they take comes out stained.

Data Dyepack plants dye packs in customer data. Canary customers mark whoever steals the data and the exact copy they took. Honey encrypted exports open to a believable fake for every wrong password. A speed alarm catches anything reading at machine speed.
